# Porter source and assumptions

This model is generated by `scripts/generate_porter_model.py` from the
`p22_description` ROS package. The source package was derived from the P-2.2
STEP assembly and is the source of truth for meshes, component placement,
mass, centre of mass, inertia and primitive collisions.

The generated simulation uses:

- eight real `MulticopterMotorModel` instances in PX4 octo-coax order;
- 5.201 kg nominal takeoff mass (rounding differs by 1 g from the source
  document's 5.202 kg budget);
- estimated V2808/T9048 propulsion coefficients from the source package;
- the reusable `model://livox_avia` sensor instead of the source's uniform
  raster lidar approximation.

The source package declares its assets proprietary. Do not redistribute the
copied meshes until their distribution terms are confirmed.
